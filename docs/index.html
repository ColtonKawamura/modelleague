Fantasy football, but for large language models.

You and a few friends draft real AI models. Every week, how those models are actually doing — benchmarks, prices, news — becomes your team's score. Two leagues: one for closed frontier models, one for open weights.

How it works

  1. You draft AI models. On draft night you and your league-mates take turns picking from a list of real models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, and the rest — until you each own five. Each model can be owned by exactly one person, so the hot ones get sniped.
  2. Every week, each model gets a score out of 100. The score — the Index — measures how good the model is right now: how well it does on the standard tests, how much it costs, whether it is getting better than last week, and whether people are using it.
  3. Your team's points are the scores of your four best models, added up. On top of that, news moves the score: a new version releases (+15), a model takes the top of a big test (+10), a price cut (+8). Penalties too: a model gets shut down (−20) or has a scandal (−10). Your fifth model sits on the bench.
  4. Most points that week wins the week. Most points over 13 weeks wins the season. That is the whole game.
  5. Optional: bet points on predictions. “Will GPT-6.1 go up or down next week?” “Which model leads the Index at the end of the season?” Bets settle automatically from the next week's data. Bragging rights and small perks — never real money.

How to get started

  1. Claim a founder spot. Each league has five slots and first come, first served: open an issue on the repo with your team name, or message Colton directly. You can run a team in both leagues if you want — it is just double the drafts.
  2. Pick a team name. Send it with your claim. A good name helps; it is the only requirement.
  3. Show up to the draft. One snake draft, five rounds, about an hour. You do not need to be an LLM expert — the Index table tells you what each model is worth at the moment.
  4. Then every Sunday a new week lands on this site: the scores, the news that caused them, the standings, and settled bets. If you play, that is your result. If you only follow, that is your model-news digest.

Not playing? The site still works as a spectator: the Index table and the weekly review are the product, and the league is the reason to come back.
